# Privacy Policy — Bucket

*Last updated: April 2026*

Bucket is a personal travel bucket-list app. We believe the smallest privacy policy
is the best privacy policy. Here is the entire one.

## What Bucket stores

Bucket keeps your saved places, notes, tags, and dream cities **only on your
device**, using your phone's local storage (AsyncStorage on iOS and Android). We
do not have a server. We do not have a database. We do not have an account
system. When you delete the app, your data is gone.

## What Bucket sends to third parties

When you search for a place inside the app, we send that search text to the
**Google Places API** so it can return autocomplete results, photos, and map pin
coordinates. Google's own privacy policy applies to that request:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>. Bucket does not enrich, log, or retain
those queries on our side, because we do not have a "our side."

When you tap "Open in Google Maps" on a saved place, your device opens the Google
Maps app (or website) with the place's coordinates. Standard Google Maps behavior
applies from that point.

## What Bucket does NOT do

- No tracking.
- No analytics.
- No crash reporting (yet — if this changes, we'll update this page).
- No advertising identifiers.
- No personal information collection: Bucket never asks for your name, email,
  phone number, birthday, photos, contacts, or location.
- No cross-app tracking.
- No data brokers.
- No accounts, so nothing to "sign up" for or "sign out" of.

## Children

Bucket is suitable for any age, but it doesn't market to or track children
specifically. Because we collect no personal data at all, we comply with the
Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by default.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that change how data is handled — analytics, cloud sync,
accounts — we will update this page and note the date at the top. The safest
way to stay informed is to check this page before upgrading.
